# Bounty Hunting

Some Dvergr stopped being *held* by the corruption a long time ago. They took the
thing inside them and made it a weapon — and unlike every other Dvergr in this mod,
they do not wait to be provoked. They hunt the roads, and they will find you before
you find them.

Those Dvergr are posted on the **Wanted Board**, and answering a posting pays.

> **This is a server feature.** Bounty hunting only runs on a server (or a local
> host) that has **BiomeLords**, **ValheimServerGuide** and **Valheim Donations**
> installed together, with the Quest pack's guidance files. Everywhere else the
> board still opens and explains itself — nothing else in the mod is affected.

## Getting started — the Warden's Commission

The board isn't open to strangers. To get in:

1. Find **Haldor**, the trader in the Black Forest.
2. Press **`Shift + E`** on him. (A *short* press of `E` still opens his store as
   normal — `Shift + E` is what starts a conversation.)
3. Hear him out and accept the commission. He marks a first bounty on your map:
   always an easy one, always close by.
4. Answer it. The board is yours from then on.

## Reading the board

Press **`F8`**, or click the **Bounty Board** button at the top of your inventory
screen.

Each posting names how dangerous the quarry is and roughly where it is. Take one and
it's marked on your map. Only one posting at a time, so choose deliberately — though
you can abandon one if you change your mind, and it goes back on the board for
someone else.

The board **rotates on its own**: unclaimed postings are retired after a while and
replaced, so there's always fresh work. A bounty you've *accepted* never expires —
it waits for you however long the trip takes.

## Hunting

Travel to the mark. Nothing is there until you arrive — then the Dvergr and its
escort appear, and they come straight for you. Harder postings bring bigger escorts
and tougher quarry.

**You can kill it, or you can free it.** Subdue it and perform the Communion Rite
exactly as you would on any Dvergr: the bounty is answered either way, the reward is
the same, and if you free it you keep it as a companion *and* collect. The board
doesn't care which you choose.

## The five tiers

| Tier | Name | Roughly where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wanted | Black Forest |
| 2 | Notorious | Swamp |
| 3 | Feared | Mountains |
| 4 | Dread | Plains |
| 5 | **Accursed** | the worst of them — see below |

Higher tiers mean more health, a bigger escort, more mages among them — and better
pay: materials that take a smelter, a forge or a cooking fire to produce, along with
coins.

## The hunters' board

Every bounty you answer scores points **weighted by difficulty**, so hunting hard
postings beats grinding easy ones. Kills and rescues are tallied separately, and both
count the same.

Standings appear on the **`F6`** ranking board alongside the duel and party ladders.
The best hunters earn **better reward bundles** for the same work.

## Accursed postings

The **Accursed** tier never appears from an ordinary posting. When one does hang on
the board, only hunters ranked in the **top 10 of the duel or party ladder** may
answer it — everyone else sees it listed and locked.

That's deliberate: the surest way to earn the right to the hardest hunts is to go and
[duel](Dvergr-Duels), or win a [tournament](Tournaments).

## If you're not on a bounty server

Press `F8` anyway. The panel explains what bounty hunting is and what a server needs
to run it. Everything else in Lost Scrolls II — companions, chores, duels,
tournaments — works exactly as normal.
