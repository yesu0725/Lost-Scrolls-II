# Wagers — Staked Tournaments & Duel Invites

Put a companion on the line for coin. Two ways to do it, both from the **Tournament panel (`F7`)**, both open to every player — no admin needed.

Everything starts the same way: **seal a companion into a [Communion Totem](Communion-Totems)** first, either at an Incinerator or in the field with a Dead Raiser. The totem is what you stake; it's held safely until the event ends and then comes home to you.

## The two currencies

| | **Coins** | **Valcoins** |
|---|---|---|
| What | ordinary Valheim coins from your pack | the server's donation currency |
| Needed | nothing | the server must run the Valheim Donations mod |

If your server doesn't support Valcoins, the Coin options still work exactly the same. The panel will tell you if a currency isn't available.

---

## Staked tournaments

**Anyone can open one.** Press `F7`, pick the currency with the slot button at the top left, and press **Start**.

- Opening costs **100 Coins** or **10 Valcoins**.
- That payment **also covers your own entry** — you still lock a totem in to claim your place, but you're never charged twice.
- The bracket is a fixed **4 players**, single elimination.
- Every other entrant pays the same fee.
- The champion wins **999 Coins** or **100 Valcoins**.

**Only one Coin tournament and one Valcoin tournament can run at a time.** You can also still enter a free tournament an admin has started — cycle the slot button to see all three.

### How it runs

1. Someone opens a tournament. It's announced in the server's Discord.
2. Players press **Lock Totem → Enter** to join, paying the fee. Each entry is announced.
3. At **4/4 the bracket begins by itself** — nobody has to start it. The draw is announced, seeded by ladder rating.
4. **You and your opponent decide where to fight.** Meet up anywhere you both like — an arena you've built, a field, a mountaintop — and each press **Ready to Fight** on the panel.
5. Once *both* of you are ready, your two companions are summoned right there, **at full health**, locked onto each other. Nobody else's companion can join in. They fight until one is subdued (nothing dies — this is a normal Dvergr duel).
6. The winner advances. Both companions are resealed into their totems, **keeping any XP they gained**.
7. When it's over: the champion takes the purse, everyone's totem comes back, and a full summary goes to Discord.

### If it doesn't fill

A tournament that never reaches 4 players is **cancelled and everything is refunded** — every stake and every totem. That happens automatically after a while, or if the host or an admin cancels it. You can also **Withdraw** at any point during registration to get your stake and your totem back in one go.

## Duel invites

A single staked duel, rather than a whole bracket.

- **Post** an invite for **100 Coins** or **10 Valcoins**, staking one sealed companion.
- **Anyone** can accept it by matching your stake and locking in their own companion.
- Both of you press **Ready to Fight** wherever you've agreed to meet, and your companions are summoned there to settle it.
- **The winner takes both stakes** — 200 Coins, or 20 Valcoins.

**You can only be in one invite at a time**, whether you posted it or accepted someone else's. Any number of *other* players can have their own invites open at the same time, and they're all listed on the panel and on **View Bracket**.

### Backing out

- **You posted it, nobody's accepted** — withdraw and get everything back.
- **You posted it, someone accepted** — withdraw and *both* of you are made whole.
- **You accepted someone else's** — withdraw and you're refunded; their invite goes back on the board for someone else.
- **The duel has started** — too late, fight it out.

An invite nobody accepts eventually expires and refunds you.

## Where to look

- **`F7` — the Tournament panel.** Everything above: open, enter, withdraw, ready up, post and accept invites. The panel spells out every cost before you press anything.
- **View Bracket** (from that panel) — a read-only board showing every running tournament, the full draw, live standings, and every posted duel invite.
- **Discord** — if your server has a webhook set up, every step is announced: a tournament opening, each player entering, the draw, each duel starting, each result, who's up next, the champion, and a full summary at the end. Duel invites are announced the same way.

## Good to know

- A duel invite's purse is **exactly the two stakes** — nothing is created, the coins just change hands.
- Your companion always fights **at full health**, however hurt it was when you sealed it.
- Duels are non-lethal, exactly like ordinary [Dvergr Duels](Dvergr-Duels) — a beaten companion is subdued, never killed.
- Wagered bouts also count on the normal duel [ladder](Rankings).
